FAQ

Questions

Got questions?

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes. No heat, no cooling, or a walk-in or freezer going warm: call 347-951-4761 any hour. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 first.

What areas do you serve?

All five NYC boroughs (Brooklyn, Queens, Manhattan, the Bronx and Staten Island), Long Island and New Jersey.

Do you work on homes or businesses?

Both. Apartments and houses, plus delis, markets, restaurants, shops and the rooftops above them.

What refrigeration do you work on?

Walk-in coolers, walk-in freezers, deli cases, open cases, glass-door fridges and the condensing units that run them.

What happens after I call?

Tell us the address and what’s going on. A tech comes out, finds the cause and explains it. You approve the price before the work starts, then we fix it or book the install.

Can I text instead of calling?

Yes. Text 347-951-4761 with the address, a photo of the unit and what’s wrong.
