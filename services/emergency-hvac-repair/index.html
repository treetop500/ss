Is this an emergency, or can it wait until morning?

Call now if:

  • There’s no heat and it’s cold out, especially with kids, older people or pipes at risk.
  • A walk-in, freezer or case is going warm with product inside.
  • Water is leaking from a unit, or there’s a burning smell from equipment.
  • A business can’t open because the space is too hot or too cold.

If you smell gas, leave and call 911 first.
