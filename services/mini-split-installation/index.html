Can I get real AC in my apartment or storefront without ductwork?

Yes. That’s what a mini split is for. An indoor unit on the wall or in the ceiling connects to an outdoor condenser through a small line set, so there’s no ductwork to run.

  • Indoor: a wall unit for a room, or a ceiling cassette for an open store floor.
  • Outdoor: the condenser goes on a wall bracket, a roof stand or the yard, wherever the building allows.
  • Heat too: heat-pump models also heat in winter.

In many NYC buildings the landlord, co-op or management has to approve outside equipment. Check that early; we can tell you what the install needs.
