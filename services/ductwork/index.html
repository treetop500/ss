Why is one room always hotter or colder than the rest?

Usually it’s airflow, not the equipment. The common causes:

  • A duct that’s undersized, crushed or disconnected somewhere you can’t see.
  • Leaky joints losing air before it reaches the room.
  • Registers that are closed or blocked by furniture.
  • A dirty filter starving the whole system.

We trace the run to that room, then seal, repair, resize or add a run, and give you the price before we start.
