My walk-in isn’t holding temperature. What should I do until you get here?

Call us first, then do what you can to slow the warm-up:

  1. Keep the door shut and make sure it closes all the way and the gasket seals.
  2. Check that nothing is blocking the fan inside the box, and the breaker for the unit hasn’t tripped.
  3. Note the temperature on the display so we know how fast it’s rising.
  4. If another cooler is holding, move the most sensitive product into it.

On site we check the condensing unit outside, the evaporator and defrost inside, and the door, then tell you what failed and what it takes to fix.
