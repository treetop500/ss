The heat’s out. What should I check before calling?

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 from outside. Don’t flip switches or try to relight anything.

Otherwise, a few quick checks:

  1. The thermostat is set to heat and above the room temperature, and its batteries aren’t dead.
  2. The boiler’s or heater’s power switch is on, and the breaker hasn’t tripped.
  3. For hot water, check whether the water heater’s pilot or display shows an error.

Still nothing? Call. We find the cause on site, whether that’s the boiler, a pump, a valve or the controls, and give you the price before we fix it.
